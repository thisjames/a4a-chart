      Birth times and places

      Most chart errors come from clock history, not from the planets. This prototype reads each country's time-zone history from the browser and handles:

      • Wartime double summer time in Britain (1941) and the all-year British Standard Time experiment (1969)
      • Local mean time before standard time existed, such as New York in 1880 and Mexico in 1907
      • Countries that used a capital's mean time as the legal time, such as Paris around 1900
      • Clock-change nights, where a time either never happened or happened twice

      What this prototype shows

      • Everything is calculated and drawn in the visitor's browser, including the PDF and PNG files, so it can sit on Squarespace with no server.
      • Free visitors see every member feature working on their own chart, with the final step locked.
      • The Preview switch flips between the two versions. On the website each page shows one.
      • Saved charts stay in this browser only.
